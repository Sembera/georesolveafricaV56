Field challenge · Working with the terrain

Steep terrain.
Experienced crews.
Controlled flight.

Steep slopes and rapidly changing ground elevations made drone magnetic surveying demanding. Maintaining visual contact with the aircraft while managing its suspended sensor required careful planning and experienced pilots.

Our team addressed these challenges through experienced piloting, maintaining visual line of sight and carefully planning and checking terrain-following flight profiles. Together, these measures helped manage terrain clearance and support more consistent survey acquisition across difficult ground.
