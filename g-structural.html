ASSESSMENT RESULT

Read the rating

Basic RMR adds strength, RQD, spacing, discontinuity condition and groundwater. Adjusted RMR applies the selected orientation penalty.

Use this as a documented classification aid. Support selection and design require the site geometry, stresses and engineering assessment.

Methods, conventions & references

Stereonet and rose diagrams

Lower-hemisphere east/north/down vectors. Equal-area radius √(1 − sin plunge); equal-angle radius tan(45° − plunge/2). Right-hand-rule strike becomes dip direction +90°. Pole trend is dip direction +180°, plunge is 90° − dip. Great circles are sampled at 1° intervals.

Rose bins start at north and use [start, end). Axial measurements reduce modulo 180° and repeat across the opposite half; percentages divide by eligible measurements once. Petal radius is proportional to count, so petal area is not proportional to frequency. Plane strike is dip direction −90°. Lines are excluded from plane roses and planes from trend roses.

RMR89

Discrete category scores, not interpolated ratings. RQD thresholds are 25, 50, 75 and 90%, with an exact threshold assigned to the higher category. Other shared endpoints are explicitly resolved in the input labels. Groundwater uses observed conditions; intact strength uses UCS in MPa. No assumed UCS conversion from rebound or point-load measurements.

A negative raw total is retained in the report and the displayed classification score is floored at zero. Missing inputs do not produce a rock class. The five joint-condition scores sum to at most 30; the overall method replaces that sum.

Rating tables: FHWA-NHI-09-010, Table 6-3, pp. 6-10–6-11; FHWA Geotechnical Site Characterization, Table 9-6. Interface references supplied: RMR Calculator and RMR Calculator Online. Structural reference: Allmendinger Stereonet.
